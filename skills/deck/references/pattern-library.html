METAFORE DECK · SLIDE PATTERN LIBRARY

Sixteen patterns. One deck.

Every slide in a Metafore deck maps to one pattern from this catalog. Pick the pattern that fits the content shape — a problem statement, a 4-up grid, a KPI band — not the prettiest layout. The thumbnails below show the region structure of each pattern; the body explains when to reach for it.

P-01Cover
Hybrid

Slide 1, always. Sets the two-tone hero. Three variants: 1A bare canvas, 1B split with glasscard, 1C split with conversational agent demo.

A 5–8 slide deck drops the agenda, the company, the integration ecosystem, and the timeline. A 20+ slide deck adds section dividers (P-02) at narrative breaks and may use the pull-quote (P-15) once. Don't repeat a pattern more than 3× in a deck. If you have four "statement" slides, three of them are probably overlap.

When in doubt

Three gut checks

Is the slide editable enough?

If the answer is "the title, but everything else is a picture" — promote the picture to a real layout (P-06 / P-07 / P-10) and let every label become a text frame.

Is the headline doing the work?

If you can remove the headline and the slide still communicates, the slide is over-illustrated. If you can remove every illustration and the slide reads fine, it's under-illustrated. Headline as the spine, illustration as the proof.

Does it look like Metafore at a glance?

Three tells: two-tone hero, Plex Mono eyebrow somewhere, single teal accent family. If a stranger could mistake it for a generic SaaS template, the brand isn't doing its job.
